FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information before you enquire.

What services do you offer?

I provide website design, Google Business Profile optimisation, local SEO, social media management, branding, printed marketing materials, personalised marketing plans and complete new business launch packages.

Do you work with new businesses?

Yes. I can help new businesses create a professional brand, website, Google presence and marketing strategy from the beginning.

Can I combine different services?

Yes. Custom packages are available for businesses that need a combination of website, Google, branding, print, social media or marketing support.

How long does a project take?

Timeframes depend on the size of the project. Smaller design projects may take a few working days, while websites and complete business launch packages may take between one and four weeks.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Social Marketeer is based in Cornwall and works with businesses throughout the UK using email, phone and online meetings.

How do I get started?

Complete the enquiry form or request a free quote. I will review your business needs and recommend the most suitable service or package.

Do you guarantee Google rankings?

No marketing company can guarantee a specific Google position. I use professional optimisation methods to improve your visibility, business information and local search presence.

Are printing costs included?

Design prices cover the creation of professional print-ready files. Printing can be arranged separately and quoted based on quantity, size and finish.
